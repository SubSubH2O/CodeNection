Here are the **judge/opponent questions** I would prepare for:

1. **Where did the 30% / 30% / 20% / 10% / 10% weights come from?**
2. **Why is 86% the threshold for "overloaded"?**
3. **What does an 80.85% load score actually mean?**
4. **How do you know your score predicts burnout?**
5. **Are you actually predicting burnout, or just measuring workload?**
6. **Why does task difficulty accurately represent mental load?**
7. **Why are good/okay/poor sleep mapped to 20%/50%/90%?**
8. **Why is daily capacity initially set to 6 hours?**
9. **How do you account for different students having different capacities?**
10. **Why is social load only self-reported if you're calling it a measured dimension?**
11. **Why do you need five dimensions instead of simply workload + wellbeing?**
12. **How did you validate that these five dimensions actually represent student burnout risk?**
13. **Where did the urgency multipliers ×1.5 / ×1.2 / ×1.0 come from?**
14. **Why should urgency increase the amount of "time" a task consumes?**
15. **What happens when there is literally no available slot to reschedule a task?**
16. **What happens if every task is marked as fixed?**
17. **What prevents users from entering inaccurate difficulty, mood, or sleep data?**
18. **How much effort does the user need to put into maintaining all this information?**
19. **Isn't asking users to constantly report their stress itself another burden?**
20. **Why should I trust the app's load score?**
21. **Why is your "AI learning" actually AI rather than a simple adaptive algorithm?**
22. **What exactly does the LLM contribute that a normal algorithm couldn't do?**
23. **Why use an LLM just to phrase recommendations?**
24. **If the important decisions are rule-based, where is the actual intelligence in your system?**
25. **How is this different from Google Calendar + a task manager + reminders?**
26. **How is this different from Motion/Sunsama/Notion?**
27. **What does your app do when the student's workload genuinely cannot be reduced?**
28. **Does recommending recovery activities actually add more things to the student's schedule?**
29. **How do you know your rescheduling suggestions actually reduce stress rather than just move tasks around?**
30. **What evidence shows that your approach actually helps prevent burnout?**
31. **Have you tested the scoring model with real university students?**
32. **How will you measure whether your app is successful?**
33. **What happens if the user's self-reported mood contradicts the calculated workload?**
34. **Why shouldn't the app simply ask the student "Are you stressed?" instead of calculating all these scores?**
35. **Could the score create anxiety by constantly telling students that they're overloaded?**
36. **Could students become dependent on the app to decide when they should rest?**
37. **How do you prevent the app from making health/mental-health claims that it cannot scientifically support?**
38. **Why did you choose self-reporting instead of automatically collecting more data?**
39. **Does privacy come at the cost of prediction accuracy?**
40. **What's the single thing your app can do that existing calendar/task apps fundamentally cannot?**
